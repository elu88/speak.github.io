Events in Ethiopia

Disputes

Amharic is Ethiopia’s national language, but over 100 other languages are spoken by different ethnic groups. Instead of bringing people together, language has sadly become another reason for division. Many Ethiopians have been forced to leave their homes, and some still can't return to the Tigray region after the war between the TPLF (Tigray Liberation Front) and the government. The humanitarian crisis is worsening, with millions relying on food aid. By supporting this app, you're not just learning a language; you're helping to keep Ethiopia’s history and culture alive.
